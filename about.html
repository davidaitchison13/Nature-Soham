About Nature Soham

Nature Soham is a community wildlife recording and conservation project focused on Soham, Cambridgeshire, and the surrounding area.

Why we’re here

Local wildlife sightings help build a picture of the plants and animals sharing our area and how the natural environment changes over time. By making it easier to record and explore sightings, Nature Soham aims to support curiosity about local nature and encourage people to care for nearby habitats.

How to take part

You can submit a wildlife sighting or browse the community sightings database. The site also brings together wildlife identification and conservation resources.

Nature Soham is a community project. Sightings are shared to support local learning and interest in wildlife.
