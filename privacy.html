Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

Nature Soham collects wildlife sighting information submitted by members of the public. To protect your privacy and comply with UK GDPR and the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, this Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, how it is protected, and your rights.

What Data We Collect

Sighting details: (Species, location, date, notes, etc.) Publicly displayed.
Name: Publicly displayed.
Email address: Stored in the private submission database and encrypted before being included in the public sightings dataset; never displayed publicly.
Phone number: Stored in the private submission database and encrypted before being included in the public sightings dataset; never displayed publicly.
Photographs: Resized before submission and stored privately in Google Drive while reviewed. With your permission, an approved photograph may be displayed with its sighting on this website.

Photographs

Photographs are optional. Submitted images are resized and converted to JPEG in your browser before they are sent to our submission service and stored in a private Google Drive folder for manual review. They are not automatically published. If you give permission and an administrator approves the photo, Nature Soham may display it with the relevant sighting on this website. This permission is non-exclusive and royalty-free; you retain the copyright. It covers display with the sighting on this website only, not other promotional use.

You can contact us through the Contact page to withdraw permission and request removal from future display. We cannot remove copies that other people may already have downloaded or shared. Please avoid including people, vehicle registration plates, or sensitive personal information in photographs.

How Your Data Is Protected

Email addresses and phone numbers are stored in a private Cloudflare D1 database, which Cloudflare says encrypts data at rest. The Worker also encrypts these values before adding a row to the GitHub-hosted sightings dataset. Only authorised administrators with access to the encryption key can decrypt those dataset values.

Only authorised administrators can decrypt and view contact details.

The public sightings database displays only your name and sighting details.

Why We Collect Your Data

We collect personal data to verify sighting submissions, contact you if clarification is needed, and maintain the integrity and accuracy of the wildlife sightings database.

Our lawful basis for processing this data is legitimate interest in maintaining a reliable community wildlife record.

How Long We Keep Your Data

Encrypted contact details are retained for as long as the sighting remains part of the Nature Soham database. You may request deletion of your personal data at any time.

Who Has Access to Your Data

Public visitors: Can see your name and sighting details only.
Administrators: Can decrypt and view email and phone number if needed.

We use Cloudflare to process submissions and store private contact details, GitHub to host the sightings dataset, and Google Drive to store submitted photographs privately while they are reviewed. These service providers process data to provide those services.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data, and request data portability.

To exercise these rights, please use the Contact page on this website.

Complaints

If you believe your data has been mishandled, you may lodge a complaint with:

Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)
www.ico.org.uk

Contact

For privacy-related enquiries, please use the Contact page on this website.
