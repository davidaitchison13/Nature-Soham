Welcome to Nature Soham

Join Nature Soham!

Are you curious about the amazing wildlife right on our doorstep in Soham?

Come be part of a friendly community passionate about discovering and protecting the natural world around us!

Explore and Record: Help us create a detailed snapshot of local wildlife to understand how our environment is changing — for better or worse.

Protect Habitats: Spot areas that need a helping hand and get involved in practical conservation activities.

Learn Together: Share tips, tricks, and useful apps for identifying plants and animals, and grow your nature knowledge.

Inspire the Next Generation: Encourage young people to connect with nature and spark their passion for wildlife.

Enjoy Nature Walks: Join seasonal guided walks along the Lode, across the commons, and other beautiful spots around Soham. You never know what you might find!

Whether you’re a seasoned nature lover or just starting out, there’s a place for you here.
